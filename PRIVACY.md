# PlayFinder Privacy Policy

PlayFinder does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No user accounts, no login, no analytics.
- Watchlist and alert preferences are stored locally on your device (DataStore) and never leave it.
- Anonymous push alerts are delivered via ntfy (https://ntfy.tedton.hk); no personal identifier is sent.
- Court availability data is fetched from the LCSD SmartPLAY open data API.
- The app displays AdMob ads; Google may process device advertising ID per its policies.
