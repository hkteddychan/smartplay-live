# PlayFinder Privacy Policy

PlayFinder does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No user accounts, no login, no analytics.
- The watchlist, the push filters and the push expiry are stored locally on
  your device (DataStore) and never leave it. Clearing the app data or
  reinstalling removes them, including any active push week.
- Push alerts are produced **on your device**: the app polls the public LCSD
  open-data feed in the background and posts a local notification. Nothing is
  sent to LCSD, to an alert broker, or to any server we control. No
  identifier, topic or subscription is registered anywhere.
- Court availability and programme activity data are fetched from the LCSD
  SmartPLAY open data API.
- Your location is used only to sort results by distance. It is read on the
  device, is never written to storage, and is never uploaded.
- The app displays AdMob ads. Google may process the device advertising ID
  and the device's IP address according to its own policies, as any app that
  serves ads does. We do not receive that information.
- A permission card appears on the Notifications tab whenever notification
  permission is missing; the system dialog is shown only if you tap it.
  Denying it leaves push unavailable; browsing is unaffected.
